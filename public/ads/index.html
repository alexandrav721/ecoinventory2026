Five frames at 1080×1350 — Instagram's 4:5 feed size, the tallest the feed allows. Each is one line and one picture, and every line is yours. No photography and no generated imagery: those are the two fastest ways to look like everyone else.

  1. You already own four umbrellas. — the one you were about to buy
  2. You paid for this in hours. — the cost in time, not money
  3. Everything here has a price tag. — the pile, tagged
  4. Your closet is a shop you already paid for. — lending
  5. Somebody on your block already owns one. — asking
To export at full resolution: open a frame, make the browser window 1080 wide, and screenshot it — or use Cmd + Shift + 4 then Space to grab the window. The markup is the master, so any of it can be changed: the line, the colours, the prices on the tags. Each frame is one small HTML file in public/ads/.
